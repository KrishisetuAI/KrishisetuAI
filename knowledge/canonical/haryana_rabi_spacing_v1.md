# Plant Spacing Norm, Sugarcane, North Indian Plains (Haryana)

## Row spacing
- Preferred row-to-row spacing: 90 cm (paired rows 120 cm wide, 60 cm between paired rows).
- Within-row plant spacing for setts: every 15-20 cm.

## Seed rate
- Approximate 8-10 tonnes of cane setts (planting material) per hectare.

## Notes
These are the 2022 edition norms; superseded by the 2024 revised spacing guidance (see the
haryana_rabi_spacing_v2 document).
