# Plant Spacing Norm, Sugarcane, North Indian Plains (Haryana) — 2024 revision

## Row spacing (revised)
- Preferred row-to-row spacing: 90 cm single rows, OR 150 cm wide paired rows at 75 cm between the
  two rows of each pair (revised from the 120/60 paired-row pattern).
- Within-row plant spacing for setts: every 15-20 cm.

## Seed rate
- Approximate 8-10 tonnes of cane setts (planting material) per hectare.

This 2024 revision supersedes haryana_rabi_spacing_v1 (2022 edition), which is not to be served.
