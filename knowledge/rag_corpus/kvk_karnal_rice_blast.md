# Rice blast management — Karnal block

## Spot the symptom
Look for spindle-shaped lesions on the leaves and dark neck blast at the panicle
base. Blast spreads fastest in cool, wet weather with high humidity.

## Varietal and cultural measures
Grow blast-resistant varieties and avoid an early, dense crop. Keep seedling
nurseries well spaced. Do not over-apply nitrogen — balanced fertility is the
first defence against blast.

## When to intervene
Water the nursery lightly rather than flood-irrigate around the neck stage.
Scout after rain. Choose a product and follow the label dose; confirm the active
ingredient and interval with the KVK agronomist before any spray. Never spray
when rain is forecast or the wind is strong.
