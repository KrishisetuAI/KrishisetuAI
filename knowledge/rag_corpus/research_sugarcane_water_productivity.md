# Sugarcane water productivity under deficit irrigation

## Study setting
A two-season field study on loam soil at Sohna compared a deficit irrigation
schedule against a fixed calendar. The objective was to hold yield while cutting
the applied water.

## Finding
Scheduling on the depletion threshold rather than a fixed interval used less
water at a small yield cost in the water-limited season. Yield loss stayed small
because the crop drew on stored soil moisture between irrigations.

## Caution
The result is site-specific and does not replace the state Package of Practices.
Extension guidance and the exact economic threshold still come from the
authoritative Tier-2 source; this note is background only.
