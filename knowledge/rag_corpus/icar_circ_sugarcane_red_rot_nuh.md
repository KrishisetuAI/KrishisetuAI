# Sugarcane red-rot management — field hygiene and resistant varieties

## Choose resistant planting material
Use varieties rated resistant to red rot for the coming season and source seed
cane only from disease-free nurseries. Avoid planting seed from a field with
visible symptoms.

## Field hygiene
Cut the crop at ground level and remove infected stubble after harvest. Do not
leave infected trash in the field; it is the primary source of inoculum for the
next crop.

## Recognize and report
Wilting of the upper leaves with reddening of the internal nodes is the classic
sign. Report any suspect field to the block agriculture officer or KVK early so
the spread can be contained before it becomes regional.
