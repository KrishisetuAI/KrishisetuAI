# Sugarcane heavy-rain advisory — Sohna block

## When an IMD heavy-rain warning is active
Suspend irrigation immediately and clear the furrow outlets so standing water can
drain before the soil waterlogs. On heavy-clay (drainage class C) fields, also
avoid field traffic while the soil is saturated.

## Action priority
| Priority | Action | Drainage note |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Suspend irrigation | All classes |
| 1 | Clear furrow outlets | Moderate and heavy clay |
| 2 | Delay foliar spray 72 h | All classes |
| 3 | Avoid field traffic | Heavy clay only |

## Foliar spraying after rain
Delay any foliar application until at least 72 hours after rain stops and the
canopy is dry. Never spray when the wind speed is high or rain is forecast within
24 hours. Consult the KVK agronomist before re-applying any product.
