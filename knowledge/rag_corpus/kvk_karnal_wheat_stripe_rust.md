# Wheat stripe-rust advisory — Karnal block

## Look for yellow stripes
Stripe rust shows as a series of yellow dashes along the leaf veins. It spreads
in cool, moist weather and can move fast across a field.

## Cultural control
Sow early and use a rust-resistant variety. Keep irrigation to the minimum
needed and avoid excess nitrogen, which makes the crop more susceptible.

## If it is detected
Report the finding to the KVK and the block agriculture officer. Scout at least
once a week through the tillering stage. Any fungicide decision should follow the
recommended active ingredient and label dose and be taken with the KVK agronomist;
do not guess a rate.
