# Sugarcane irrigation under water stress — Palwal block

## When to irrigate
Irrigate when the 7-day accumulated rainfall has fallen short of the district
reference evapotranspiration and soil moisture in the root zone is heading toward
the depletion threshold. Use the district ET0 figure from the IMD agromet
outlook rather than a fixed calendar.

## Volume per irrigation
Apply roughly 75 mm per irrigation during grand growth. On sandy-loam (drainage
class S) the interval is shorter; on heavy clay (class C) it is longer. Stop
irrigation well before harvest to lift sucrose.

## Interval table
| Soil class | Interval guide | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Sandy loam (S) | 7 days | 75 mm |
| Loam / clayey (M) | 10 days | 75 mm |
| Heavy clay (C) | 12 days | 75 mm |

## Under prolonged stress
Prioritize the youngest fields first. Where canal water is short, rotate the
available water and keep a written irrigation log so the KVK can advise on the
next cycle.
