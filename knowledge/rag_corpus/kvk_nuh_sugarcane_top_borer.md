# Sugarcane top-borer monitoring — Nuh block

## Scouting frequency
Walk the field every week from tillering onward. Look for dead-heart symptoms and
bore holes at the top of young canes, and record the percentage of affected shoots.

## Cultural control
Remove and destroy infested shoots. Keep the field free of grass weed hosts and
avoid excessive nitrogen, which attracts the pest.

## Chemical control
Only intervene once the economic threshold is clearly crossed. Confirm the active
ingredient and the exact label dose with the KVK agronomist before any
application; do not rely on a generic rate. Follow the approved interval and
re-entry period, and never spray within 24 hours of forecast rain.
