# Sugarcane ratoon management — gap filling and rotation

## When to preserve the ratoon
A ratoon with a stand gap of 20% or less should be kept. Manage trash as mulch,
shave stubble to encourage uniform sprouting, and fill only the very poor gaps.

## When to selectively gap-fill
Between a 20% and a 40% gap, fill only the missing stools and keep the field.
Monitor tiller count and maintain soil moisture through the tillering stage.

## When to replant or rotate
A gap above 40% points to replanting. But if the ratoon is already a third ratoon
or older and viability has fallen below the economic threshold, a rotation break
is the better spend: a legume green-manure such as dhaincha followed by maize
restores the field rather than spending on replanting a failing ratoon.

## Practical rule of thumb
| Gap in row | Decision |
|---|---|
| up to 20% | Preserve and manage |
| 20% to 40% | Selective gap fill |
| above 40% | Replant or rotate if uneconomic |
